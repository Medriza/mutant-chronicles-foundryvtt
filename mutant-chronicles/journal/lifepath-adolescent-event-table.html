Adolescent Event Table

Roll 2d20 and add the results together. You may spend one Life point to re-roll or, with GM approval, pick the result. Results provide a suggested character Trait and an optional effect.

Roll Adolescent Event Suggested Trait Optional Effect

* Criminal Record: Characters with a Criminal Record reduce their Social Status by one, and may only select first Primary Careers in Column A or Column B. Characters with a Criminal Record may automatically select Criminal or Military (Basic) careers for free, and may never select a police or corporate executive career.
