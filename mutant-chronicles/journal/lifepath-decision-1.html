Decision One: Attributes

A character's life begins with attributes determined by a range of factors that influence his early life. The Lifepath will take your character through a series of events that will determine his starting Attributes. A character begins with all characteristics (Agility, Awareness, Co-ordination, Intelligence, Mental Strength, Personality, Physique, and Strength) at 5.

A character may voluntarily lower any number of Attributes by one point (to 4) and assign these points to other Attributes. A player may spend Life points to raise an Attribute by one point for each Life point spent. No Attribute can start the Life Path greater than 6.

Example: Mick decides he wants a tough shooter type character, so he reduces both his Intelligence and Personality scores to 4, and assigns these points to his Co-ordination and Strength, increasing those scores to 6.

See Character Makeup and Decision Eight for information on Attributes and how these affect your starting wounds, ranged weapon, close combat, and influence bonuses (Derived Stats).

Important: This decision must be made now and the points assigned to Attributes now — you cannot choose to delay this choice until later.
