Decision Five: Education

What type of education did your character receive and what Attribute bonuses, skills, talents, and equipment do they gain?

Step One

Roll 1d6 and consult Column A of the Education Table below. You may pay one Life point to pick (or roll) from Column A or Column B. You may pay two Life points to pick (or roll) from Column C.

Roll Column A (Free) Column B (1 Life Point) Column C (2 Life Points)

Cybertronic characters: May not choose Brotherhood Educated or Brotherhood Apprenticeship. If either result is rolled, Brotherhood Educated becomes Technical Pre-Career Training and Brotherhood Apprenticeship becomes Post Graduate Scientific.

Step Two

Make a note of the various education effects for your character. See the Education Reference page for the full entry for your education type.

  • Attribute Improvements: Increase each of your Attributes by the values listed.
  • Mandatory Skills: You receive one rank of training in all of these skills. If this is the first rank in a skill, gain Expertise 1. If you already have Expertise 1, you may use this rank to gain either Expertise or Focus ranks.
  • Elective Skills: Pick two of these skills; you gain one rank of training in each.
  • Talents Available: Select one of the eligible skills and one talent from the associated tree, provided you fulfil the prerequisites.
  • Career: You may choose this career without paying Life points or rolling on the Primary Career table. You may also roll on the Primary Career table and then choose to take your education's career option instead.
  • Equipment: You may take this piece of equipment or asset.

Step Three

Once you have determined your education, roll 2d20 and add the results together to see what has affected your upbringing on the Adolescent Event Table (see next page). You may spend one Life point to re-roll or, with GM approval, pick the result.

Some results provide suggested character Traits, which can be triggered to gain Chronicle points (see Chapter 9: Chronicles Points). Each event can also trigger optional effects that allow you to customise your character further.
