Decision Four: Environment

In this section, you will learn what type of environment your character grew up in, and how this influenced him.

Step One

Roll 1d6 and consult the Environment Table below. A character may pay one Life point to choose the result.

Once you have determined the type of environment, choose a single Attribute from the two listed and increase it by one. In addition, you receive one rank of training in the skill listed for that environment.

The Region Table (see next page) provides examples of what each environment type represents for each faction.

Step Two

Based on your environment, you also receive a single choice from the Regional Equipment Table below. Each environment entry has one or more items of equipment listed, one of which may be chosen.

Environment Equipment (Pick One)
Luna CityA basic urban survival kit (poncho for acid rain, air filter mask, etc).
Heritage WorldA favour owed by a connection in your own faction.
Heritage FootholdA basic survival kit for your region, or a basic medkit.
Heritage HotspotEither a heavy civilian shoulder pad, a corporation-appropriate handgun, or a basic medkit.
Orbital / Minor Heritage LocationA vacuum suit or colonial survival kit for your region.
SequesteredHigh society clothing, a notable object of personal significance (relic, piece of art, etc), a meditation room and a personal library.
